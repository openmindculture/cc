## Colors and Typography Ideas

The wine plus gold pairing is the most durable part of a palette, because it comes straight from book-cover tradition. The deep teal gives a scientific, instrument-like counterpoint without feeling trendy.

What matters more than the palette

Typography. A well-chosen serif for text, with a quiet sans for UI details, ages better than any color decision. Avoid fonts that are currently fashionable.
Restraint with color. Aim for roughly 90% paper and ink, with accent colors appearing only in small doses, such as links, rules, or a section marker.

Let the book covers carry the color. If the artwork is the vivid element, the site should stay quiet around it. That also keeps the site from competing with new cover designs later.
